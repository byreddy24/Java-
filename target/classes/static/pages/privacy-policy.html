Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2, 2025

At TeachRise, your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control your data.

Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, email address, password, and profile information provided when you register.
  • Course Activity: Courses enrolled, learning progress, assessments, and certificates achieved.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device information, browser type, and cookies for login/session security and improving user experience.
  • Communications: Messages, support requests, and feedback.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our learning services.
  • To communicate updates, progress, promotions, and critical information.
  • To improve platform performance, security, and your learning experience.
  • To issue certificates for completed coursework.

How We Share Information

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We may share limited data with instructors, accreditation partners, or technical providers solely to deliver service to you.
  • We comply with legal/regulatory requirements if demands are lawfully made.

Your Rights & Choices

  • View, update, or delete your profile information at any time in your account settings.
  • Unsubscribe from email communications using the link at the bottom of any marketing email.
  • Request complete deletion of your data by emailing support@teachrise.com.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for core site functionality (login/session) and anonymized analytics only. You may disable cookies in your browser; however, some features may not function as intended.

Data Security

Your data is encrypted and securely stored. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests about this policy, please contact our team at support@teachrise.com.
