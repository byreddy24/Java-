    Help & Support

    Welcome to the SkillSpark Help Center! Find answers to common questions or reach out to our support team for assistance.

    To enroll, navigate to the Browse Courses section, select a course you're interested in, and click Enroll. You may need an active membership depending on the course.
    Click on the Profile icon in the top right, go to Settings, and select Change Password. Follow the instructions to update your password securely.
    You can email us at support@skillspark.com or use our live chat feature during business hours for immediate assistance.
    We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for membership and course purchases.
    Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase with no more than 10% course progress. Please review our Refund Policy for details.
